↓

Our showroom is located at Varangode, Kizhakkethala, Malappuram, Keralam 676519. Conveniently situated near Kizhakkethala junction with ample customer parking. You can navigate directly via Google Maps Link.

What are your showroom timings throughout the week?

↓

CODE is open Every Day from Monday to Sunday, 9:30 AM to 10:00 PM. We remain open on public holidays to accommodate busy wedding shopping schedules.

Do you customize synchronized attire for groomsmen and family?

↓

Yes, this is one of our flagship specialties! We design coordinated wedding sets, matching suits, and sherwani complements for entire wedding parties (3 to 12+ members) in requested color palettes. You can consult directly with our master stylists on WhatsApp or visit our Varangode showroom!

How early before my wedding date should I visit for fitting?

↓

While we carry a wide assortment of ready-to-wear tuxedos and blazers that can be altered within 24–48 hours, for bespoke made-to-measure tailoring and intricate hand zardozi embroidery we recommend visiting 2 to 4 weeks prior to the wedding date.

Can I consult on WhatsApp before visiting or from the Gulf?

↓

Absolutely! You can chat or book video calls directly with our styling consultants at +91 79079 39090. Send us your preferred color theme or reference designs to check availability immediately.
